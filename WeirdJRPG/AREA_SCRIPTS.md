## AREA 1: THE BRASS DREAMSCAPE

### OVERVIEW

The first area is a steampunk dreamscape where the physical and the impossible coexist. It's where the Pilgrim's journey begins.

### VISUAL DESCRIPTION

```
The Brass Dreamscape is a world of golden gears, brass platforms, and floating islands. Steam rises from vents in the ground, creating pockets of warm, golden fog. The sky is a perpetual sunset, with the sun always setting in the distance.

The architecture is impossible: towers that spiral into infinity, bridges made of clockwork mechanisms, and doors that lead to different times and places. Everything is made of brass, gold, and steam.

The air hums with the sound of machinery, the ticking of clocks, and the singing of clockwork birds. Occasionally, gravity fails, and objects float upward into the sunset.
```

## AREA 2: THE BETWEEN-SEA

### OVERVIEW

A vast ocean that exists between realities. It's both water and consciousness.

### VISUAL DESCRIPTION

```
The Between-Sea is an ocean that is also a mind. The water is translucent, and you can see through it to other realities. The waves are not water, but memories and emotions given form.

The sky is a mirror, reflecting everything below. The horizon is never reached, only more sea. The water hums with the sound of voices, whispers, and the ocean's deep song.

Occasionally, the sea becomes solid, and you can walk on it. Or it becomes air, and you can fly through it. Or it becomes... something else.
```

### KEY LOCATIONS

#### 1. THE LIGHTHOUSE OF LOST THINGS

**Description**: A lighthouse that guides lost souls.

**Features**:
- The light is not light, but consciousness
- Beings come to the lighthouse, seeking guidance

## AREA 3: THE STONE-DEEP

### OVERVIEW

The core of the Void-Realm, where the Stone of Herself is embedded.

### VISUAL DESCRIPTION

```
The Stone-Deep is a crystalline world of impossible geometry. The structures are made of crystal that glows with colors that hurt to look at. The void is visible in patches, pure nothingness where reality doesn't exist.

The central feature is the Stone itself — a massive, pulsating crystal that is actually you. The Guardians are beings of pure crystal, multiple forms, ancient and cosmic.

The air hums with the sound of crystal resonating, the void's presence, and the Stone's heartbeat. Occasionally, reality breaks down, and you see the code, the math, the structure holding everything together.
```

### KEY LOCATIONS

#### 1. THE CRYSTAL SPIRE

**Description**: The main structure of the Stone-Deep.

**Features**:
- Crystal structures that are impossible
- The spire spirals upward into the void
- Crystal light illuminates everything
- The spire is actually a being

**Dialogue**:

```
NPC: The Crystal Guardian
"STEAM CRACKLES OFF THE OPEN FIRE. WHO?? WHO???"

THE CARROT IS SPARED AND THE STICK KNOWS ME TOO WELL. IS THAT ALL THERE IS? CHEMICAL REACTIONS? IS IT AN EXPLOIT? OR DO YOU MEAN TO TELL ME IF I POUR SOME SALT ONTO A BUNDLE OF PROTEINS IT'S JUST GONNA START FEELING IT TOO"

**Dialogue**:

#### 4. THE FINAL CHOICE

**Description**: The area where the player makes their final decision.

**Features**:
- Three paths, each representing a choice

## AREA 4: THE YEAR 1977

### OVERVIEW

A side area that exists outside time, where the world was in 1977.

### VISUAL DESCRIPTION

```
The Year 1977 is a retro-futuristic world where 1970s technology meets cosmic horror. Everything is yellow, orange, and electronic green. The teeth of everyone are yellow, representing the world's decay.

The architecture is a mix of 1970s brutalism and cosmic horror. The sky is a perpetual orange sunset. The air hums with the sound of 1970s music and cosmic horror.

The inhabitants are beings who don't remember why they exist. They tell stories to cope with the horror.
```

### KEY LOCATIONS

#### 1. THE YELLOW TOWN
