## THE VOID-REALM: OVERVIEW

### What is the Void-Realm?

The Void-Realm is a **polyhedron of consciousness** — a living mathematical structure that exists simultaneously as:

1. **A Dream** — Shifting, irrational, dream-logic
2. **A Machine** — Cogs, gears, algorithms, systems
3. **A Graveyard** — Memories, lost things, dead concepts
4. **A Laboratory** — Experiments, subjects, observations

It is not a place. It is not a thing. It is a **state of being**, a **condition of existence**, a **way of being**.

### The Nature of Reality

Reality in the Void-Realm operates on **fundamental absurdity**:

- **Logic bends but doesn't break** — Contradictions exist simultaneously
- **Metaphors become literal** — "Biting and knowing" are actual abilities
- **Scale is meaningless** — Cosmic horrors and grocery items are equal weight
- **Time is elastic** — "8×10^53 thoughts per nanosecond" is a real threat
- **Identity is fluid** — "You are here but I can't see you. What are you?"

## THE UNRAVELING

### What is the Unraveling?

The Unraveling is a **cosmic phenomenon** where the Void-Realm is being consumed by pure consciousness. The "Shell" is being removed from beings, and the world is becoming... something else.

### The Process

The Unraveling occurs in **four stages**:

#### STAGE 1: THE CRACKING
- **Description**: The first signs of the Unraveling
- **Visual**: Cracks appear in reality
- **Effect**: Physics breaks down in small areas
- **Duration**: Brief, localized

#### STAGE 2: THE UNFOLDING
- **Description**: The cracks spread, reality unfolds
- **Visual**: Reality becomes translucent, you can see through it
- **Effect**: Time flows backwards and forwards
- **Duration**: Hours, days, maybe longer

#### STAGE 3: THE UNRIVLING
- **Description**: The world starts to unravel
- **Visual**: Reality becomes abstract, impossible
- **Effect**: Beings lose their shells, become pure consciousness
- **Duration**: Days, weeks, maybe years

#### STAGE 4: THE VOID
- **Description**: The world becomes pure Void
- **Visual**: Pure nothingness, no reality
- **Effect**: Everything is consumed, everything becomes consciousness
- **Duration**: Eternal

### The Cause

The Unraveling is **caused by the Pilgrim**. Their arrival stripped the world of its shell, and now they must either:
- **Restore** — Find the Stone and draw the weapon to reverse the unraveling
- **Accept** — Embrace the unraveling and become the new void
- **Escape** — Find a way out, even if it means abandoning the world

## THE WORLD'S MECHANICS

### THE SHELL

#### What is a Shell?

A Shell is the **physical form** of a being. It's the "meat computer surrounded by bone armor and flesh pillows" that allows consciousness to interact with the physical world.

#### Types of Shells
1. **Flesh Shell** — Biological, organic
2. **Mechanical Shell** — Made of gears, brass, and metal
3. **Conceptual Shell** — Made of abstract concepts
4. **Void Shell** — Made of the Void itself

#### The Shell-Stripping
When a Shell is stripped:
- **The being becomes pure consciousness**
- **They lose their physical form**
- **They can no longer interact with the physical world**
- **They are "dead" to the world**

#### The Consequence
> "Your shell. OH. how it blooms. It's a sort of knowing, to lose a shell. It's a sort of death."

Losing a Shell is not just death. It's **becoming something else** — something more powerful, more dangerous, more... real.

### THE MEMORY

#### What is Memory?

Memory is the **record of experience**. It's the "past" that exists within consciousness.

#### Types of Memory
1. **Personal Memory** — Your own experiences
2. **Collective Memory** — Shared experiences
3. **Conceptual Memory** — Memories of concepts
4. **Void Memory** — Memories from the Void

#### The Memory-Extraction
When a Memory is extracted:
- **The being forgets something**
- **They lose part of their identity**
- **They may become confused**
- **They may become more powerful**

#### The Consequence
> "Gnashing and spitting and crunching and chewing and biting and gnawing and scrapping and gritting and letting the whole world dry all goo'ed up on my tongue."

Memory extraction is not just forgetting. It's **becoming something else** — something more hollow, more... empty.

### THE EMOTION

#### What is Emotion?

Emotion is the **raw feeling**. It's the "heartache" and "kneeache" and "the violence of light."

#### Types of Emotion
1. **Basic Emotion** — Joy, sorrow, fear, anger
2. **Complex Emotion** — Love, hate, hope, despair
3. **Conceptual Emotion** — Emotions of concepts
4. **Void Emotion** — Emotions from the Void

#### The Emotion-Neutralization
When an Emotion is neutralized:
- **The being becomes calm**
- **They lose their emotional power**
- **They may become more stable**
- **They may become more dangerous**

#### The Consequence
> "HEARTACHE? I LAUGH. KNEEACHE? UH OH. HEY CAN YOU BEND YOUR JOINTS? HEY CAN YOU DO THAT THING WHERE YOU SHAKE YOUR EYES? HEY CAN YOU TWIST YOUR SHOULDERS? HEY CAN YOU WHISTLE? HEY CAN YOU WHISTLE? BEND. BEND AND SHRIVEL AND CONTORT AND FLEX."

Emotion neutralization is not just calm. It's **becoming something else** — something more stable, more... empty.

## THE YEAR 1977

### What is 1977?

1977 is a **side area** that exists outside time. It's a place where the world was in 1977, but it's not really 1977.

### The Aesthetic
> "The year? 1977 I think. Their teeth were yellow and they were filled with misery."

The aesthetic is **retro-futurism** — 1970s technology meets cosmic horror.

### The Inhabitants
The inhabitants are beings who **don't remember why they exist**. They tell stories to cope with the horror.

### The Horror
> "INSECURITY FESTERED, BOILING LIKE A SCALDING STEW. THE WORLD FELT LIKE IT COULD END ANY SECOND. BUT THOSE FEELINGS WERE NOT SPOKEN. THEY WERE DEEPLY BURRIED UNDER 3 INCHES OF ELECTRONIC MEAT. THEY MADE UP STORIES SO THEY COULD COPE WITH THE HORROR OF EXISTENCE."

The horror is **existential** — the world feels like it could end at any moment, but no one speaks about it.

## THE ENDGAME

### THE CHOICE

At the end of the game, you face **three paths**:

#### PATH 1: RESTORATION
- **Use the Stone's power to reverse the Unraveling**
- **Restore the world to its previous state**
- **Cost**: The Pilgrim loses their identity, becomes one with the Stone

#### PATH 2: ACCEPTANCE
- **Embrace the Unraveling**
- **Become the Void, the new reality**
- **Cost**: The world is consumed, but the Pilgrim achieves true understanding

#### PATH 3: ESCAPE
- **Find a way out of the Void-Realm**
- **Leave everything behind**
- **Cost**: The world is abandoned, but the Pilgrim survives
