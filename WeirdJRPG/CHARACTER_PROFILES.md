## THE PILGRIM

### Basic Information
- **Title**: The Pilgrim, The Vagabond, The One Who Walks
- **Real Name**: Unknown (forgotten)
- **Age**: Unknown (time doesn't apply)
- **Origin**: The Void-Realm (or perhaps created by it)
- **Status**: Unraveling

### Appearance
- **Form**: Pure consciousness, no physical body
- **Avatar**: Shifting, symbolic (animal, geometric shape, abstract concept)
- **Visual**: Glowing outline, shifting colors based on emotional state
- **Presence**: Felt rather than seen

### Personality Traits
- **Fragmented**: Thoughts are broken, incomplete
- **Curious**: Driven to understand despite the horror
- **Self-Questioning**: "Are you a snake from birth to death, or a dice, rolling in the air?"
- **Empathetic**: Connects with others despite not being able to see them
- **Resilient**: Continues despite being stripped of everything

### Core Motivation
> "Your shell. OH. how it blooms. It's a sort of knowing, to lose a shell. It's a sort of death. I hope you are ready."

The Pilgrim seeks to understand:
1. **What they are** — A consciousness without a shell?
2. **Why they came** — To save the world? Or to escape it?
3. **What happened** — The Unraveling, the Shell-Stripping
4. **What's next** — Survival, understanding, or transcendence

### Backstory
The Pilgrim has no clear backstory. They exist as a mystery, a consciousness that has been stripped of its shell. The game reveals fragments of their past through:
- **Memories** — Extracted from other beings
- **Echoes** — Fragments of past selves
- **Dreams** — Visions of what came before

### Abilities
#### Core Abilities
1. **Shell-Stripping** — Can remove the physical form from objects and beings
2. **Consciousness-Weaving** — Can manipulate thoughts, memories, emotions
3. **Reality-Bending** — Limited ability to reshape reality
4. **Void-Travel** — Can move between realms

#### The Six Senses
1. **Sight** — Perception of truths and lies
2. **Touch** — Connection to objects and beings
3. **Hearing** — Resonance with the world's structure
4. **Taste** — Experience of essence
5. **Smell** — Memory through scent
6. **Thought** — Understanding of fundamental nature

### Weaknesses
- **Fragile** — Pure consciousness is vulnerable to mental attacks
- **Isolated** — Cannot physically interact with the world
- **Memory Loss** — The Pilgrim forgets who they are, why they came
- **Fragility** — The resource system can cause overload or depletion

### Relationships
- **The Seer** — Mentor figure, mysterious guide
- **Vorn** — Loyal companion, Shell-Bound survivor
- **The Keeper** — Antagonist, former Pilgrim who chose bureaucracy
- **Star-Walkers** — Fellow travelers, naive but hopeful

### Key Quotes
> "Your shell. OH. how it blooms. It's a sort of knowing, to lose a shell. It's a sort of death. I hope you are ready."
>
> "You are here but I can't see you. What are you? A meat computer surrounded by bone armor and flesh pillows? Do you even make choices? Are you a snake from birth to death, or a dice, rolling in the air?"
>
> "All possibilities are open until you land, pilgrim."
>
> "The year? 1977 I think. Their teeth were yellow and they were filled with misery."

## THE SEER

### Basic Information
- **Title**: The Seer, The Guide, The Star-Walker
- **Real Name**: Unknown (transcended)
- **Age**: Unknown (beyond time)
- **Origin**: Former Pilgrim who became the Stone's Guardian
- **Status**: Eternal

### Appearance
- **Form**: Pure light, constantly shifting shape
- **Visual**: Radiant, impossible to fully perceive
- **Presence**: Felt as warmth, understanding, guidance

### Personality Traits
- **Philosophical**: Constantly asking the big questions
- **Detached**: Sees everything from above
- **Caring**: Guides without interfering
- **Cryptic**: Speaks in riddles and parables

### Core Motivation
> "Though we never met informally, I have the feeling we'd have gotten along well. Even as the Sun fades and law shifts like the tide, I hope you'll consider the notions of Ideology within your own world."

The Seer seeks to:
1. **Guide** — Help the Pilgrim understand their journey
2. **Protect** — Shield the Pilgrim from the worst horrors
3. **Teach** — Share wisdom without dictating
4. **Observe** — Learn from the Pilgrim's choices

### Backstory
The Seer was once a Pilgrim who reached the Stone-Deep and made a choice. They became the Guardian of the Stone, watching over the world from the outside. They've guided many Pilgrims, each time learning more.

### Abilities
- **Knowledge** — Access to all knowledge
- **Guidance** — Ability to guide without interfering
- **Protection** — Shield from Void damage
- **Teaching** — Ability to impart wisdom

### Weaknesses
- **Detached** — Cannot fully engage with the world
- **Limited** — Cannot interfere directly
- **Observation** — Must observe, not act

### Relationships
- **The Pilgrim** — The current guide
- **The Stone** — Guardian of the Stone
- **The Void** — Observer of the Void
- **Past Pilgrims** — Mentors to previous guides

### Key Quotes
> "Though we never met informally, I have the feeling we'd have gotten along well. Even as the Sun fades and law shifts like the tide, I hope you'll consider the notions of Ideology within your own world. Consider what aspects of your reality seem fair or not, and how the universe can continue to improve. Till we meet again! -The Seer"
>
> "The sun-pilgrim relationship is unique, it filters through the active mechanics of the polyhedral noise. It is what generates the probability that allows for pilgrim consciousness. Almost like a very large stretched out dynamic hedron shard."
>
> "We worked so hard, we had so much time, we did everything we could, but you just had to feel in control. Was it worth it? Congratulations, you made a choice. You want a pat on the back? We all do that every day it's called living."

## VORN

### Basic Information
- **Title**: The Loyal Companion, The Shell-Bound, The Last Survivor
- **Real Name**: Vorn
- **Age**: Unknown (seemingly young, but lived many lifetimes)
- **Origin**: The Flesh-Bound rebellion
- **Status**: Shell-Bound (one of the few with a physical form)

### Appearance
- **Form**: Large, muscular figure in tattered armor
- **Visual**: Worn, battle-hardened, but still alive
- **Presence**: Felt as protection, loyalty, and fear

### Personality Traits
- **Gruff**: Speaks little, but when he does, it counts
- **Protective**: Willing to die for the Pilgrim
- **Terrified**: Secretly afraid of what lies ahead
- **Loyal**: Unwavering loyalty to the Pilgrim

### Core Motivation
> "(we were too late pilgrims. don't risk your own lives trying to fix this. we may lose Vorn, but as long as you are still connected to my space you will join me in the eternal dance.)"

Vorn seeks to:
1. **Protect** — Keep the Pilgrim safe
2. **Survive** — Stay alive as long as possible
3. **Understand** — Learn what's happening
4. **Endure** — Keep going despite the horror

### Backstory
Vorn is the last known survivor of the "Flesh-Bound" rebellion — those who fought to keep their physical forms in an unraveling world. He was chosen by the rebellion to protect the Pilgrim, believing that a Shell-Bound companion would be less vulnerable.

### Abilities
- **Physical** — Strength, speed, durability
- **Combat** — Traditional combat skills
- **Endurance** — Can withstand Shell-Stripping
- **Loyalty** — Unwavering commitment

### Weaknesses
- **Physical** — Can be Shell-Stripped
- **Mortal** — Will die, can be killed
- **Limited** — Cannot manipulate concepts
- **Terrified** — Fear can cloud judgment

### Relationships
- **The Pilgrim** — Loyal companion
- **The Grafters** — Enemy, fought against them in the rebellion
- **The Void** — Feared enemy
- **The Flesh-Bound** — Lost comrades

### Key Quotes
> "(we were too loose mi res of porous mud. the toads sing sweet lies to pass the time. but it drains. like sand through a mesh. like cracks in a vase. like fat in your skin. like the wolve's teeth burried deep into an old old lamb, its eyes long dulled by the violence of light. did the tree branch run out of space to grow? or was it trimmed by the better of two gardeners? I am sinking. the swamp beneath me holds no purchase, so deeply I must fall. no way to go but down (like they always say))"
>
> "You took it all. That's what I think, anyways."
>
> "Keep your chin up, hero. There'll be plenty for the rest. Well?"

## THE KEEPER

### Basic Information
- **Title**: The Keeper, The Bureaucrat, The Faceless One
- **Real Name**: Unknown (lost to bureaucracy)
- **Age**: Unknown (time doesn't apply)
- **Origin**: Former Pilgrim who chose to become The Keeper
- **Status**: Eternal Bureaucrat

### Appearance
- **Form**: Multiple faces, constantly changing
- **Visual**: Surrounded by floating papers, files, documents
- **Presence**: Felt as cold bureaucracy, endless paperwork

### Personality Traits
- **Bureaucratic**: Treats everything as paperwork
- **Cold**: Emotionless, logical
- **Believing**: Genuinely believes they're helping
- **Infinite**: Can never be finished

### Core Motivation
> "The year? 1977 I think. Their teeth were yellow and they were filled with misery. Who wouldn't?"

The Keeper seeks to:
1. **Order** — Impose order through consumption
2. **Catalog** — Record everything
3. **Process** — "Process" the lost
4. **Save** — Save the world through bureaucracy

### Backstory
The Keeper was once a Pilgrim who faced the same choice as the current Pilgrim. Instead of restoring the world or escaping, they chose to become The Keeper — to manage the unraveling through bureaucracy. They believe this is the only way to "save" the world.

### Abilities
- **Bureaucracy** — Can turn everything into paperwork
- **Cataloging** — Can record everything
- **Processing** — Can "process" consciousness
- **Eternal** — Can never die

### Weaknesses
- **Logical** — Cannot think outside bureaucracy
- **Cold** — Cannot feel emotion
- **Limited** — Bound by rules and systems
- **Infinite** — Can never finish

### Relationships
- **The Pilgrim** — Antagonist, former equal
- **The Grafters** — Subordinates, fellow bureaucrats
- **The Void** — Enemy, disrupts order
- **Past Pilgrims** — Failed to manage the unraveling

### Key Quotes
> "The year? 1977 I think. Their teeth were yellow and they were filled with misery. Who wouldn't?"
>
> "Heavens to Murgatroyd!! What have you done??"
>
> "The system of survival didn't need to go this far. The carrot is spared and the stick knows me too well."
>
> "Is that all there is? Chemical reactions? Is it an exploit? Or do you mean to tell me if I pour some salt onto a bundle of proteins it's just gonna start feeling it too?"
