## QUICK REFERENCE

### CORE MECHANICS

#### The Six Senses
1. **Sight** — Perception of truths and lies
2. **Touch** — Connection to objects and beings
3. **Hearing** — Resonance with the world's structure
4. **Taste** — Experience of essence
5. **Smell** — Memory through scent
6. **Thought** — Understanding of fundamental nature

#### Concept Combat
1. **Strip** — Remove the shell
2. **Extract** — Pull out a specific concept
3. **Absorb** — Take a concept into yourself
4. **Weave** — Combine concepts
5. **Unmake** — Remove an enemy entirely

#### Fragility Resource
- **Gain**: Extracting concepts, damaging enemies
- **Spend**: Using abilities, surviving attacks
- **Overload**: Too much Fragility — reality breaks down
- **Depletion**: Too little Fragility — you fade away

### ENDGAME CHOICES

#### PATH 1: RESTORATION
- Use the Stone's power to reverse the Unraveling
- Restore the world to its previous state
- Cost: The Pilgrim loses their identity, becomes one with the Stone

#### PATH 2: ACCEPTANCE
- Embrace the Unraveling
- Become the Void, the new reality
- Cost: The world is consumed, but the Pilgrim achieves true understanding

#### PATH 3: ESCAPE
- Find a way out of the Void-Realm
- Leave everything behind
- Cost: The world is abandoned, but the Pilgrim survives

## TONE AND STYLE

### Narrative Voice
- **Fragmented**: Broken thoughts, incomplete sentences
- **Questioning**: "What are you?"
- **Self-Reflective**: "It was wellworthit. A sort of bargain of nothing."
- **Scientific**: "The sun-pilgrim relationship is unique, it filters through the active mechanics of the polyhedral noise."
- **Bureaucratic**: "The year? 1977 I think. Their teeth were yellow and they were filled with misery."
- **Existential**: "You are here but I can't see you. What are you?"

### Writing Guidelines
- **Use ALL CAPS for emphasis, not shouting**
- **Fragmented sentences**
- **Scientific absurdity**
- **Pop culture + cosmic horror**
- **Physical sensations as metaphors**

## GAMEPLAY LOOP

1. **Explore** — Unfold the map, discover new areas

4. **Fight** — Combat using concept manipulation
5. **Collect** — Gather shells, memories, emotions, names, concepts
6. **Unfold** — The game itself unfolds as you play
