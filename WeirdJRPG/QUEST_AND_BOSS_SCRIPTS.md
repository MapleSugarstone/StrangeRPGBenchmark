## MAJOR QUESTS

### QUEST 1: THE UNRAVELING (Main Quest)

#### Description
The primary quest that drives the game. The Unraveling is consuming the Void-Realm, and you must understand and stop it.

#### Objective
- Understand the Unraveling
- Find the Stone of Herself
- Draw the Weapon
- Make the Choice

#### Key Items
- **The Stone of Herself** — The source of the Unraveling
- **The Weapon** — The power to reverse the Unraveling
- **The Shell** — Your own shell, lost and found

#### Progression
1. **Discovery** — You witness the first signs of the Unraveling
2. **Understanding** — You learn what the Unraveling is
3. **Journey** — You travel through the Void-Realm
4. **Confrontation** — You face the source of the Unraveling
5. **Choice** — You make your final decision

#### Key Dialogues

```
Opening:
"The Unraveling... it's here. I can feel it. The world is... unraveling."
"The yogurt, the spogurt, the wild, the skreeb, the falsehood fire and the scoba feeb. There is a sort of: Wire. There is a sort of: Nothing."

Middle:
"Your shell. OH. how it blooms. It's a sort of knowing, to lose a shell. It's a sort of death. I hope you are ready."
"Burning and knowing and biting and- there is nothing left. You took it all."

End:
"It was wellworthit. A sort of bargain of nothing. But we are rich, yes?"
"Though we never met informally, I have the feeling we'd have gotten along well. Even as the Sun fades and law shifts like the tide, I hope you'll consider the notions of Ideology within your own world. Consider what aspects of your reality seem fair or not, and how the universe can continue to improve. Till we meet again! -The Seer"
```

## BOSS FIGHTS

### BOSS 1: THE FIRST WATCHMAN (Brass Dreamscape)

#### Appearance
A massive brass construct with a face that changes constantly.

#### Health
1000 Fragility

#### Attacks
1. **Brass Storm** — Throws brass fragments at you
2. **Shell-Stripping** — Strips your shell, leaving you vulnerable
3. **Gear-Grind** — Grinds gears, causing physical damage

#### Weakness
Memory-Extraction — Removing its memories

#### Fight Flow
```
Phase 1: The Watchman attacks with brass fragments
Phase 2: It strips your shell, testing your defenses
Phase 3: It reveals its memories, you extract them
Phase 4: It becomes vulnerable, you unmake it

Combat Dialogue:
"You took it all. That's what I think, anyways."
"Burning and knowing and biting and- there is nothing left."
```

#### Defeat
The Watchman is stripped to its core, becoming a simple brass statue.

### BOSS 2: THE ARCHIVIST'S ARCHIVE (Between-Sea)

#### Appearance

#### Health
2000 Fragility

#### Attacks
1. **Paper Storm** — Throws papers at you
2. **Face-Flood** — Faces appear everywhere
3. **Bureaucratic Overload** — Overwhelms you with paperwork

#### Weakness

#### Fight Flow
```
Phase 1: The Archive attacks with paperwork
Phase 2: Faces appear, confusing you
Phase 3: It reveals its bureaucratic power, you neutralize it
Phase 4: It becomes vulnerable, you unmake it

Combat Dialogue:
"The year? 1977 I think. Their teeth were yellow and they were filled with misery."
"Heavens to Murgatroyd!! What have you done??"
```

### BOSS 4: THE GUARDIAN (Final Boss)

#### Appearance
A being of pure crystal, multiple forms.

#### Health
Variable (based on your choice)

#### Attacks
1. **Crystal-Strike** — Attacks with crystal
2. **Void-Touch** — Touches the Void, consuming you
3. **Memory-Flood** — Floods you with memories
4. **Emotion-Storm** — Attacks with emotions

#### Weakness
The Player's Choice — The boss is defeated based on your decision

#### Fight Flow
```
Phase 1: The Guardian attacks with crystal
Phase 2: The Void touches, you're destabilized
Phase 3: Memories flood, you're overwhelmed
Phase 4: Emotions attack, you're consumed
Phase 5: The Choice — You decide the Guardian's fate

Combat Dialogue:
"HEARTACHE? I LAUGH. KNEEACHE? UH OH."
"THOUGH WE NEVER MET INFORMALLY, I HAVE THE FEELING WE'D HAVE GOTTEN ALONG WELL."
```

#### Defeat
The Guardian's fate depends on your choice:
- **Restoration**: The Guardian becomes one with the Stone
- **Acceptance**: The Guardian becomes the Void
- **Escape**: The Guardian is abandoned

Consider what aspects of your reality seem fair or not, and how the universe can continue to improve. Till we meet again! -The Seer"
