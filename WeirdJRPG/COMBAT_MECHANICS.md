# COMBAT MECHANICS BIBLE
## The Concept Combat System

---

## PHILOSOPHY

Combat is not about hitting things. It's about **manipulating concepts** — stripping, extracting, and weaving the fundamental aspects of reality.

> "Burning and knowing and biting and- there is nothing left. You took it all."

---

## CORE MECHANIC: THE SIX SENSES

The Pilgrim operates through six senses, each tied to a different concept:

### SIGHT (Perception)
**Passive**: See hidden truths, lies, and the world's underlying structure
**Active**: True Sight — See the world as it truly is
**Ultimate**: Unseeing — Make something impossible to perceive

### TOUCH (Connection)
**Passive**: Feel the emotional state of nearby beings
**Active**: Handshake — Connect with an object or being
**Ultimate**: Embrace — Merge with something, temporarily

### HEARING (Resonance)
**Passive**: Hear the world's "hum," its underlying structure
**Active**: Echo — Repeat or alter sounds
**Ultimate**: Silence — Create absolute silence, disrupting reality

### TASTE (Experience)
**Passive**: Taste the essence of things
**Active**: Savor — Extract the "flavor" of something
**Ultimate**: Devour — Consume everything around you

### SMELL (Memory)
**Passive**: Smell the memories of places
**Active**: Scent — Follow the "scent" of something
**Ultimate**: Oblivion Scent — Make something forgettable

### THOUGHT (Understanding)
**Passive**: Understand the fundamental nature of things
**Active**: Insight — See the truth
**Ultimate**: Awakening — Become aware of something's true nature

## COMBAT FLOW

### PHASE 1: APPROACH
The Pilgrim moves to the enemy. No traditional attacks, only **conceptual approach**.

**Movement Types**:
- **Walk** — Normal movement
- **Slide** — Quick, vulnerable movement
- **Float** — Move through the air, immune to ground attacks
- **Weave** — Move through enemies without harming them

### PHASE 2: CONCEPT EXTRACTION
The player chooses what to extract from the enemy:

#### SHELL
**Description**: The physical form
**Effect**: Enemy loses physical form, becomes a shadow
**Risk**: Enemy becomes ungraspable

#### MEMORY
**Description**: Emotional/mental power
**Effect**: Enemy forgets something crucial, becomes confused
**Risk**: Enemy may become more powerful

#### EMOTION
**Description**: Raw feeling
**Effect**: Enemy becomes unbalanced, possibly more powerful
**Risk**: Enemy may become uncontrollable

#### NAME
**Description**: Identity
**Effect**: Enemy is "forgotten," may disappear or become something else
**Risk**: Enemy may become a void

### PHASE 3: CONSEQUENCE
Extracting something has consequences:

| Extraction | Consequence |
|------------|-------------|
| Shell | Enemy loses physical form, becomes a shadow |
| Memory | Enemy forgets something crucial, becomes confused |
| Emotion | Enemy becomes unbalanced, possibly more powerful |
| Name | Enemy is "forgotten," may disappear or become something else |

## ABILITY TYPES

### STRIP
**Description**: Remove the shell, leave a hollow shell behind
**Use**: Makes enemies vulnerable, creates physical objects
**Resource Cost**: Low Fragility
**Cooldown**: Short

### EXTRACT
**Description**: Pull out a specific concept (memory, emotion, name, etc.)
**Use**: Gain resources, weaken enemies
**Resource Cost**: Medium Fragility
**Cooldown**: Medium

### ABSORB
**Description**: Take a concept into yourself, gain power
**Use**: Heal, gain abilities, become more powerful
**Resource Cost**: High Fragility
**Cooldown**: Long

### WEAWE
**Description**: Combine concepts to create new abilities
**Use**: Create hybrid abilities, powerful combos
**Resource Cost**: Very High Fragility
**Cooldown**: Very Long

### UNMAKE
**Description**: Remove an enemy entirely from existence
**Use**: Permanently defeat enemies
**Resource Cost**: Extreme Fragility
**Cooldown**: Very Long

---

## RESOURCE SYSTEM: FRAGILITY

### Description
Fragility represents how "real" something is. It's both a resource and a danger.

### GAIN FRAGILITY
- Extracting concepts from enemies
- Damaging enemies
- Successful concept manipulation

### SPEND FRAGILITY
- Using abilities
- Surviving attacks
- Resisting effects
- Exploring unknown areas

### OVERLOAD
**Trigger**: Having too much Fragility
**Effect**: Reality starts breaking down around you
**Consequences**:
- Environment becomes unstable
- Physics breaks down
- You start to forget who you are

### DEPLETION
**Trigger**: Having too little Fragility
**Effect**: You fade away
**Consequences**:
- You become intangible
- You lose your memories
- You risk disappearing entirely

## COMBAT ENEMY TYPES

### SHELL-BOUND
**Description**: Beings with physical forms
**Weakness**: Shell-Stripping
**Example**: Brass Golem, Clockwork Beast

**Behavior**:
- Physical attacks
- Defensive stances
- Can be damaged

**Strategy**: Strip their shell first, then extract other concepts

### MEMORY-STRUCK
**Description**: Beings whose memories have been attacked
**Weakness**: Memory-Extraction
**Example**: Forgotten Soldier, Lost Child

**Behavior**:
- Confused, erratic attacks
- Forgetful, vulnerable
- Can be exploited

**Strategy**: Extract their memories to break them down

### EMOTION-FED
**Description**: Beings powered by raw emotions
**Weakness**: Emotion-Neutralization
**Example**: Rage-Beast, Sadness-Walker

**Behavior**:
- Emotional attacks
- Unpredictable
- Can be overwhelmed

**Strategy**: Neutralize their emotions, then exploit

### CONCEPTUAL
**Description**: Beings made of abstract concepts
**Weakness**: Concept-Unmaking
**Example**: Idea of Nothing, Concept of Silence

**Behavior**:
- Abstract attacks
- Reality-breaking
- Can be unmade

**Strategy**: Unmake the concept that defines them

### VOID-Touched
**Description**: Beings that have touched the Void
**Weakness**: Void-Resistance
**Example**: Void-Walker, Nothing-Eater

**Behavior**:
- Void attacks
- Reality-shifting
- Can consume you

**Strategy**: Protect yourself from Void damage

## COMBAT STAGES

### STAGE 1: THE STRIP
**Objective**: Remove the enemy's physical form
**Difficulty**: Easy
**Goal**: Make the enemy vulnerable

### STAGE 2: THE EXTRACT
**Objective**: Extract a specific concept
**Difficulty**: Medium
**Goal**: Weaken the enemy

### STAGE 3: THE UNMAKE
**Objective**: Remove the enemy from existence
**Difficulty**: Hard
**Goal**: Defeat the enemy permanently

## COMBAT EXAMPLES

### EXAMPLE 1: THE BRASS GOLEM
**Appearance**: A massive brass construct
**Shell**: Brass and gears
**Memory**: Memories of its creator
**Emotion**: Pride and fear
**Name**: "The Watchman"

**Combat Flow**:
1. **Strip**: Remove the brass shell, leave hollow gears
2. **Extract**: Pull out its memories of the creator
3. **Unmake**: Unmake its concept of "protection"
**Result**: The Golem becomes a pile of gears, then fades away

### EXAMPLE 2: THE FORGOTTEN SOLDIER
**Appearance**: A soldier without a memory
**Shell**: Tattered uniform
**Memory**: All lost, fragmented
**Emotion**: Confusion and longing
**Name**: "The Lost"

**Combat Flow**:
1. **Strip**: Remove the uniform's shell
2. **Extract**: Extract the soldier's remaining memories
3. **Unmake**: Unmake the concept of "soldier"
**Result**: The Soldier becomes a blank figure, then fades away

### EXAMPLE 3: THE RAGE-BEAST
**Appearance**: A beast made of pure rage
**Shell**: None (it's all emotion)
**Memory**: None (rage has no memory)
**Emotion**: Pure, unbridled rage
**Name**: "The Angry"

**Combat Flow**:
1. **Strip**: Impossible, it has no shell
2. **Extract**: Extract its rage
3. **Unmake**: Unmake the concept of "rage"
**Result**: The Beast becomes calm, then fades away

## COMBAT VARIATIONS

### VARIANT 1: THE GROUP FIGHT
**Description**: Fighting multiple enemies at once
**Challenge**: Managing multiple concepts
**Strategy**: Focus on one enemy, then move to the next

### VARIANT 2: THE ENVIRONMENTAL COMBAT
**Description**: Using the environment in combat
**Challenge**: The environment is hostile
**Strategy**: Turn the environment against the enemies

### VARIANT 3: THE BOSS FIGHT
**Description**: Fighting a powerful enemy
**Challenge**: Multiple stages, high Fragility cost
**Strategy**: Understand the boss, exploit their weaknesses

## COMBAT PROGRESSION

### LEVEL 1: THE BEGINNER
- Can only Strip shells
- Limited Fragility management
- Basic combat

### LEVEL 2: THE LEARNER
- Can Extract memories

### LEVEL 3: THE PRACTITIONER
- Can Extract emotions and names
- Advanced Fragility management
- Can use all ability types

### LEVEL 4: THE MASTER
- Can Weave concepts
- Expert Fragility management
- Can Unmake enemies

### LEVEL 5: THE VOID-WALKER
- Can manipulate the Void itself
- Perfect Fragility management
- Can become anything
