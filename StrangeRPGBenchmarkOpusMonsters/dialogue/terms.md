# Key terms

Every invented or repurposed term the game uses, with what it means, how many lines of game text use it, and names the Naturalizer suggests instead. Fresh names come from asking for clearer or more natural names. Plain names come from asking for everyday words a ten-year-old would understand. Counts are lines in `dialogue/whorl-text.txt` by set (story, interface, rules), so a rename with a high count touches many lines.

Mark the names you want in the Pick column, or write your own. A rename goes in before the full naturalize pass, so the pass works on the new words.
