# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Prayer Cards

== nq_cards_start
> face hero up
(PRAYER CARDS. ONE GILT. OUT OF ORDER.)
(FIRST PRINTINGS WANTED. GOOD PRICE. ASK AT THE CANDLE STALLS.)
PORT: Cards with her image? Where can I find them?
> quest nq_cards 1

== nq_cards_pay
> if pcard_plaza&pcard_terraces&pcard_steps&pcard_spire&pcard_church&pcard_sunny -> all
Candles: First printings? Six to a set. Bring me six, and I'll pay for six.
> shop candles
> end
* all
Candles: Six. All first printings. The ink ran. The rain does that.
Candles: Turn them over. The backs hold a recipe: eel soup. Nobody's read it in forty years.
> lore eel_soup
?
- Sell them. A hundred and twenty gilt. -> sell
- Give them to the pilgrims' children. They'll want her on paper. -> give
- Keep them. They're mine now. -> keep
* sell
> take prayercard 6
> gilt 120
Candles: Pleasure. They'll go in a frame on the Brim. Nobody up there will read the back.
> rep church 1
> goto done
* give
> take prayercard 6
Candles: Free? To children? ...The Saint would like that. I'll say it was the Church.
> rep church 2
> approve ignace 1 courtesy 1
> goto done
* keep
Candles: Keep them, then. Make the soup. Bring me a bowl.
* done
> set nq_cards_done
> quest nq_cards done

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- The Kettle

== nq_kettle_start
> face hero up
> sfx wake
PORT: It sings. Softly. Is it singing to us?
(A paper tag on the handle reads: IF FOUND, MEVROUW LINDEMAN, TERRACE 2.)
?
- Take it to her. -> take
- Call Recovery. They pay for woken property. -> recovery
- Leave it on the sill. -> leave
* take
> give kettle
> set nq_kettle_started
> quest nq_kettle 1
PORT: It's warm. It sings louder now. Are we sad?
> end
* recovery
> sfx pa
Board: Recovery thanks you for reporting woken property. Thirty gilt credited.
> gilt 30
> rep board 1
> rep woken -1
> set nq_kettle_started
> set nq_kettle_hushed
> ifnot party:tova -> fin
Tova: thirty gilt. for a kettle. that sang.
> approve tova -2
> end
* leave
* fin

== nq_kettle_lind
> face lindeman hero
> if nq_kettle_done -> after
> ifnot item:kettle -> none
Lindeman: That's my kettle. It blew out the window in the spring. It goes where it's needed.
Lindeman: It sang for my husband while he was dying. For three years. Then it went looking for someone else.
Lindeman: It sings when it finds someone sad. Who's sad, then? You?
?
- It's yours. Take it home. -> home
- It found me. I'd like to keep it. -> keep
- What would you sell it for? -> sell
* sell
Lindeman: Sell it? It isn't mine to sell anymore. It chose.
?
- Then take it home. -> home
- Then I'll keep it. -> keep
* home
> take kettle
> sfx wake
> wait 1
Lindeman: Listen. It stopped. It's home, so it stopped.
PORT: It stopped. Because it got here. Will I?
> set kettle_home
> rep woken 1
> goto done
* keep
Lindeman: Then it's yours. Put it near a window. It likes to see who's coming.
> set kettle_kept
> approve tova 1
* done
> set nq_kettle_done
> quest nq_kettle done
> end
* none
Lindeman: Have you seen a tin kettle? It sings. It went out the window in spring.
> end
* after
> if kettle_home -> h
Lindeman: Is it still singing? Then someone near you is sad. Look after them.
> end
* h
Lindeman: It's on the stove. It hasn't made a sound since. I make tea with it anyway.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Sloop or Gerda

== nq_bet
> face haas hero
> if nq_bet_done -> after
Haas: The Bailers. Sloop and Gerda, racing from the bend to the bridge. Twenty gilt stakes you forty.
> quest nq_bet 1
?
- $20 Sloop. -> sloop
- $20 Gerda. -> gerda
- [Notice 2] Watch them for a moment first. -> watch | blind
- Betting on the Bailers means betting the Deeps will stay dry. No. -> no
* watch
Haas: Then just watch. The clock is ticking.
> anim bailer
> wait 1
PORT: The larger one limps. It's its left foot. The other one doesn't.
?
- $20 Gerda. The one who doesn't limp. -> win
- $20 Sloop. She's always been that way. -> lose
* blind
Haas: Watching won't help. It's the Bailers.
?
- $20 Sloop. -> sloop
- $20 Gerda. -> gerda
- Pass. -> no
* sloop
> check notice 1 -> win | lose
* gerda
> check notice 1 -> win | lose
* win
> anim bailer
> wait 1
Haas: The bridge! That's the bridge. Forty, as promised.
> gilt 40
> goto done
* lose
> anim bailer
> wait 1
Haas: The bridge. Not yours. Twenty to the house.
> goto done
* no
Haas: The Deeps are wet whether I bet or not, love.
> ifnot party:quaye -> done
Quaye: Twenty gilt to watch giants walk. Waste of time.
> approve quaye 1
* done
> set nq_bet_done
> quest nq_bet done
> end
* after
Haas: Same time tomorrow, love. They never miss.

> end

?

> if song_crypt -> c

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Forty-Four

== nq_44_start
> face hero up
> sfx wake
PORT: The button breathes. It wants us to press it. I can feel it wanting.
> quest nq_44 1
?
- Press forty-four. -> press
- ?item:hush Hush it. -> hush
- [Hack 2] Make the lift skip forty-four forever. Let the button rest. -> skip | press
- Leave it. -> leave
* press
> sfx door
> wait 1.5
(The lift ascends and halts. The doors slide open to reveal an empty corridor, a window, and the sea.)
PORT: It stopped. It arrived. Look. The button is still.
> gilt 30
> give bigstim 1
> rep board -1
> set nq_44_pressed
> goto done
* hush
> take hush
PORT: It's cold. It stopped wanting. I didn't like that.
> rep woken -1
> set nq_44_hushed
> goto done
* skip
> sfx hack
PORT: It doesn't need to want anymore. It's just a button again. Is that kind?
> set nq_44_skipped
> goto done
* leave
> end
* done
> set nq_44_done
> quest nq_44 done

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Dead Letters

== nq_letters_start
> face hero left
(Two envelopes in the bin, both stamped RETURNED. ADDRESS UNKNOWN.)
(TO THE GARDENERS OF TERRACE 4. TO JET, BOX 7, LANTERN STEPS.)
?
- Take them. I know where both of those are. -> take
- Open them first. -> open
- Leave them. -> leave
* open
(The first thanks the Terrace 4 gardeners for tomatoes that accidentally reached the Brim.)
(Twenty gilt notes, folded inside the letter.)
(The second: SORRY ABOUT THE MIC. I WOKE IT ON A BAD NIGHT. IT WAS NEVER MEANT TO STAY SAD. A.)
> lore tomato_bread
?
- Keep the twenty. Deliver the rest. -> keepmoney
- Put it all back and deliver both. -> take
* keepmoney
> gilt 20
> set nq_letters_skimmed
* take
> give letter_gardener
> give letter_jet
> quest nq_letters 1
> end
* leave

== nq_letter_gardener
> take letter_gardener
Gardener: From the Brim? For us? ...Tomatoes reached the Brim. Packed in a Board crate.
> if nq_letters_skimmed -> skim
Gardener: She sent twenty gilt. For the co-op. That's a month's worth of seed.
> rep deeps 1
> goto check
* skim
Gardener: She says she sent money. There's no money here. Must have been a Board post.
* check
> goto nq_letters_check

== nq_letter_jet
> take letter_jet
Jet: A letter? For me? Nobody writes to Box 7.
Jet: ...It's from whoever woke the mic. Sorry, it says. It was a rough night.
Jet: I'll read it into the mic. Maybe it'll inspire a happy song one day.
> goto nq_letters_check

== nq_letters_check
> if item:letter_gardener|item:letter_jet -> fin
> set nq_letters_done
> quest nq_letters done
* fin

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Two Hundred and Ten

== nq_argue
> face vos hero
> if nq_argue_done -> after
ClerkVos: Two hundred and ten meters. The plaque says so.
ClerkMol: Two hundred and eleven. They added a meter after the Flood. Nobody bothered to change the plaque.
ClerkVos: We've had this argument for eleven years. Settle it. You climbed it, didn't you?
> quest nq_argue 1
?
- Two hundred and ten. The plaque is right. -> ten
- Two hundred and eleven. Plaques lie. -> eleven
- [Notice 2] Count the sea-facing platforms. Do the math. -> count | guess
- {quaye} Quaye? -> quaye
* quaye
Quaye: Two hundred and ten and a half. The extra half is sandbags. I signed for them.
ClerkVos: ...A half.
ClerkMol: A half. Eleven years.
> approve quaye 2
> goto half
* count
PORT: Four platforms. Fifty-two and a half each. I counted. Is that right?
* half
ClerkVos: Two hundred and ten and a half. Both wrong. Both pay.
> gilt 30
> goto done
* guess
> goto eleven
* ten
ClerkVos: Ha. Ten. The plaque. Fifteen gilt, as agreed.
> gilt 15
> goto done
* eleven
ClerkMol: Eleven! The extra meter! Fifteen, and my eternal respect.
> gilt 15
* done
> set nq_argue_done
> quest nq_argue done
> end
* after
ClerkVos: We have a new argument now. Whether the sea is getting closer.
ClerkMol: It is.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- The Seawalk Clock

== nq_clock_start
> face hero up
> if item:clockkey -> wind
(A brass clock in the planter wall. Stopped at a quarter past nine. The night of the Flood.)
(A keyhole under the face. A plate: KEY HELD AT RECEPTION.)
> quest nq_clock 1
> end
* wind
PORT: The key. We can make it go. Which time do we set it to?
?
- Set it to now. Let it run. -> now
- Wind it, but leave the hands at a quarter past nine. -> leave
* now
> take clockkey
> sfx confirm
PORT: Tick. Tick. It's moving. Was it waiting forty years?
> rep board 1
> set clock_running
> goto done
* leave
> take clockkey
PORT: It ticks, but stays frozen at the Flood. Like someone who refuses to forget.
> rep deeps 1
> set clock_flood
* done
> set nq_clock_done
> quest nq_clock done
