== camp2_ambrose
> music camp
> walk ambrose @camp_ambrose
> face ambrose up
> walk hero @camp_hero
> face hero ambrose
Ambrose: My eye, on the free tier, recites dictionaries when I can't sleep.
Ambrose: Tonight it's the letter P. Pluvial. Relating to rain.
?
- Tell me about the knee. -> knee
- Who sponsored you before? -> sponsor
- How much do you still owe? -> owe
* knee
Ambrose: A gift from a man named Haas. Delivered in the eleventh round of a very long night.
Ambrose: It locks when it rains. And it rains every night. You can see the difficulty.
> goto close
* sponsor
Ambrose: A brewery. When my knee gave out, they went with it. Gently. By letter.
> goto close
* owe
> if ambrose_debt_paid -> paid
Ambrose: Four hundred. Bodywell sends a polite reminder to my eye every hour.
> goto close
* paid
Ambrose: Nothing, this month. A novel condition. I am savoring it.
> approve ambrose 1
* close
> wait 1
Ambrose: Pluvial. A fine word. I shall use it until it begs me to stop.
