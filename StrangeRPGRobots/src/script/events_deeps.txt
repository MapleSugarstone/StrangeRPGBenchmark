# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Background: the canal runner's Nine Bells

== ninebells_talk
> face wessel hero
> if ninebells_done -> after
> if ninebells_on -> on
Wessel: You've won the Nine Bells twice. Gus says. Gus says everything.
Wessel: It's tonight. The Pound to the Ferry Graveyard, lantern in hand, before the nine bells.
Wessel: Mara Vos is running. Her mum's chrome arm is unpaid. She needs the purse.
> quest ninebells 1
?
- I'll run it. -> run
- Mara can have it. I'll sit this one out. -> sit
- What's the purse this year? -> purse
* purse
Wessel: Two hundred. And your name's burned into the Pound fence. That's the real prize.
?
- I'll run it. -> run
- Not this year. -> sit
* sit
Wessel: Oh. Right. Mara wins, then. Probably.
> set runner_gave
> set ninebells_done
> rep deeps 1
> quest ninebells done "You sat out the Nine Bells. Mara Vos won it."
> end
* run
Wessel: The lantern's at the Pound, by Tilly's corner. Go when you're ready.
> set ninebells_on
> quest ninebells 2
> end
* on
Wessel: The lantern's at the Pound. Tilly's corner. Mara's already there.
> end
* after
> if runner_won -> won
> if runner_cheated -> cheat
Wessel: Mara's mum got her arm back. Mara says thanks. She won't say it to you.
> end
* won
Wessel: Three times. Your name's on the fence three times now.
> end
* cheat
Wessel: Everybody knows about the boat. Nobody says it. Everybody knows.

== ninebells_start
> face hero up
> if ninebells_ran -> done
> set ninebells_ran
> enter mara mara @loop_ne left
> walk mara @bin_spot
> face mara hero
Mara: You're actually running? You. Okay. Fine.
Mara: Don't trip me on the canal near the Board. I'll trip you back.
(The Pound bell tolls eight. One bell for the Ferry Graveyard.)
?
- [Athletics 2] Take the long way, along the boardwalks. Fair and fast. -> fair | slow
- $20 Take the Lockjaw cut. Pay the toll, take the short water route. -> cut
- [Sneak 2] The canal along the Board, right under the checkpoint. The fastest route. -> board | caught
- Let Mara take the lead. And let her keep it. -> give
* fair
> set nb_fair
> goto finish_win
* slow
> goto finish_lose
* cut
> set runner_cheated
> rep lockjaws 1
> rep deeps -1
> goto finish_win
* board
> set runner_cheated
> rep board -1
> goto finish_win
* caught
> rep board -1
Officer: Swimmer in the Board canal. Get out of the water, please. What's your name?
> goto finish_lose
* give
> set runner_gave
> rep deeps 2
> goto finish_lose
* finish_win
> fade out 0.8
> exit mara
> warp ferries @taxi up
> enter mara mara 3 31 right
> walk mara 6 31
> face mara hero
(You reach the ferry mud with your lantern lit, a boat's length ahead.)
Mara: You won it. Fine. Mum's arm can wait a month.
> gilt 200
> ifnot runner_cheated -> fairwin
Mara: Everybody saw the boat. Don't think they didn't.
> goto close
* fairwin
> set runner_won
> rep deeps 2
?
- Take the purse, Mara. For your mum's arm. -> purse
- Next year, Mara. -> keep
- Two hundred's two hundred. -> keep
* purse
> gilt -200
> set runner_gave
> rep deeps 2
Mara: You won it and you're giving it away. That's the worst thing anyone's ever done to me.
Mara: Thank you.
> goto close
* keep
Mara: Next year. I'll be faster next year.
> goto close
* finish_lose
> fade out 0.8
> exit mara
> warp ferries @taxi up
> enter mara mara 6 31 left
> face mara hero
(Mara hits the ferry mud first, lantern held high, already winded.)
> ifnot runner_gave -> lost
Mara: You let me. I saw you let me. I'm taking it anyway.
> goto close
* lost
Mara: Ha. Twice is enough for anyone.
* close
> set ninebells_done
> quest ninebells done
> end
* done
(The race is over.)
