# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Paid Up (market)

== niche_meter
> if meter_paid|meter_cracked -> after
(A parking meter on the bridge road. The flag says EXPIRED.)
(It flips to PAID as you lean in. Then it drops back.)
> if meter_seen -> ask
> set meter_seen
> quest meter 1
NOTICE: The slot is wider than a gilt. It takes the old money, from before the Flood.
> ifnot party:courtesy -> ask
Courtesy: PAYMENT. IS. REQUIRED.
* ask
?
- ?item:halfgilt Feed it the old half-gilt. -> pay
- [Strike 2] Crack the case. Forty years of coins went in. -> crack | crackfail
- Feed it a gilt. -> gilt
- Leave it. -> leave
* gilt
(The gilt drops through and rolls out at the bottom.)
(EXPIRED.)
> end
* pay
> take halfgilt
> sfx confirm
(The half-gilt coin drops in. Something chimes inside, once.)
(The flag rises to PAID and stays.)
> wait 1.5
(It never flips back.)
> set meter_paid
> approve courtesy 2
> xp 100
> quest meter done "The meter got its half-gilt. The flag stays on PAID."
> end
* crack
> sfx melee
> shake 1
(The case splits open. Coins spill across the boardwalk, a mix of gold and old currency.)
(PAID. EXPIRED. PAID. Then the flag lingers halfway.)
> sfx loot
> gilt 60
> set meter_cracked
> approve courtesy -3 ambrose 1
> quest meter done "You cracked the meter for sixty gilt. Its flag hangs halfway."
> end
* crackfail
> sfx hit
(The case rings out and holds. EXPIRED, EXPIRED, EXPIRED, flashing rapidly.)
> end
* leave
> end
* after
> if meter_cracked -> broke
(PAID. It has read PAID since the night you fed it.)
> end
* broke
(The meter hangs open and empty. The flag rests halfway.)
> end

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Docking Song (ferries)

== niche_horn
> if horn_sung|horn_kept|horn_stripped -> after
(A foghorn from the wheelhouse of a sunken ferry. Brass, green with corrosion at the seams.)
> if horn_seen -> ask
> set horn_seen
> quest horn 1
> sfx pa
(One long note. Then a pause, lasting as long as a single line of a song.)
> wait 1.5
> sfx pa
(The note sounds again.)
NOTICE: It leaves space for words. It wants the docking song sung back.
* ask
?
- ?song_a&song_b&song_c Sing all three lines. -> sing
- ?song_a&song_b&song_c Sing two lines. Keep the last one. -> keep
- Unscrew the brass. Forty gilt to anyone with a boat. -> strip
- Leave it. -> leave
* sing
(Toss the rope. The post is close.)
(Watch your step.)
(Nine bells have rung, and no one has drowned.)
> wait 1
> sfx pa
(The horn sounds its final note. It is lower than the others.)
> wait 2
(It does not sound again.)
PORT: No one drowned. I'll keep that one.
> set horn_sung
> xp 100
> quest horn done "You sang the docking song back. The foghorn went quiet."
> end
* keep
(Toss the rope. The post is close.)
(Watch your step.)
> wait 1.5
> sfx pa
(The horn sounds, leaves a pause, then waits.)
> set horn_kept
> rep lockjaws 1
> quest horn done "You left the foghorn wanting its last line. It still sounds at nine bells."
> end
* strip
> sfx melee
(The bolts are softened by salt. The horn comes away warm in your hands.)
(It tries to sound. Air escapes from the end, but no note comes.)
> sfx loot
> gilt 40
> set horn_stripped
> approve courtesy -2
> quest horn done "You sold the foghorn's brass for forty gilt."
> end
* leave
> end
* after
> if horn_kept -> waits
> if horn_stripped -> holes
(The foghorn is green and quiet.)
> end
* waits
(The foghorn sounds, leaves a pause, then waits.)
> end
* holes
(Four bolt holes in the wheelhouse roof.)
> end

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- The Slow Race (pound)

== niche_race
> if race_done -> after
(A chalkboard hangs on a lamp post. TONIGHT: CART 2 TO 1. PRAM 9 TO 1.)
(Beneath it sits a biscuit tin, its lid cut with a slot.)
> quest race 1
(A shopping cart and an old pram roll up to a chalk line on the tram loop.)
?
- [Notice 2] Watch a lap first. -> watch | bet
- Put ten on the cart. -> cart
- Put ten on the pram. -> pram
- Not tonight. -> leave
* watch
NOTICE: The cart rattles as if angry. The pram rolls as if asleep.
NOTICE: At the canal, the cart stops to stare at the water. Every single lap.
* bet
?
- Put ten on the cart. -> cart
- Put ten on the pram. -> pram
- Not tonight. -> leave
* cart
> ifgilt 10 -> cart2
(The tin waits. Your pockets are empty.)
> end
* cart2
> gilt -10
> set race_cart
> goto run
* pram
> ifgilt 10 -> pram2
(The tin waits. Your pockets are empty.)
> end
* pram2
> gilt -10
> set race_pram
* run
(The cart speeds off. The pram rolls away slowly.)
> wait 1.5
(At the canal, the cart stops. It stares at the water for a long time.)
> wait 1.5
(The pram rolls past and crosses the line.)
> if race_pram -> won
> set race_done
> quest race done "The pram won. The cart stopped at the canal, the way it does every lap."
> end
* won
(Ninety gilt in the tin: coins, buttons, and a single ring.)
?
- Leave it in the tin. The Pound takes its cut for lamp oil. -> give
- Take the ninety. -> take
* take
> sfx loot
> gilt 100
> approve courtesy -1
> goto close
* give
> gilt 10
> rep woken 2
> approve courtesy 2
* close
> set race_done
> quest race done "The pram won. The cart stopped at the canal, the way it does every lap."
> end
* leave
> end
* after
(TONIGHT: CART 2 TO 1. PRAM 9 TO 1. The odds haven't shifted.)
> end

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Return to Sender (vein)

== niche_post
> if post_done -> after
(A red postbox clings to the fish market wall, swollen with letters. Its flap is sealed tight.)
> if post_seen -> ask
> set post_seen
> quest post 1
(Suddenly, the flap opens. It bites your fingers, then snaps shut.)
(The door at the bottom has rusted open just a finger's width. Envelopes, dozens of them.)
NOTICE: Every single one is addressed to someone who sailed with the Vein. Names. Streets.
> ifnot party:sallis -> ask
Sallis: Ten years of names. Recovery pays per name.
* ask
?
- ?party:sallis|rep:vein>=3 Send them out on the next Vein boat. -> send
- Burn them here. -> burn
- Recovery pays four gilt a name. Count them. -> sell
- Leave them. -> leave
* send
> ifnot party:sallis -> send2
Sallis: Copy. Next boat out. They'll be late.
* send2
(A fish crate swallows ten years of letters.)
> set post_sent
> rep vein 2
> approve sallis 3 courtesy 1
> quest post done "The letters went out on the next Vein boat."
> goto close
* burn
(The letters arrive quickly. The postbox stays warm for an hour.)
> set post_burned
> rep vein 1
> approve sallis 1
> quest post done "You burned the letters. Nobody can read the names now."
> goto close
* sell
(Thirty names. A Recovery runner on the bridge counts them twice.)
> sfx loot
> gilt 120
> set post_sold
> rep vein -4
> rep board 1
> approve sallis -6 courtesy -2
> quest post done "Recovery paid a hundred and twenty for the letters."
> goto close
* leave
> end
* close
> set post_done
> end
* after
(The postbox is empty. The flap flaps uselessly.)
> end

Noud: I need a blue one, a green one, and a brown one with letters on it. Old city glass.

> if item:glassblue&item:glassgreen&item:glassbrown -> have
> end

?

> goto close

> take glassblue
> take glassgreen
> lore board_bottle

> end

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- The Candle Raft (flood)

(It bumps the school wall. Again. Again.)

NOTICE: Every flame leans the same way, up the road, toward the Wall.
> ifnot party:ignace -> ask
Ignace: Somebody bled on a shrine while they prayed. It's trying to get to her.

?
- [Athletics 2] Wade in and push it clear of the school. -> push | pushfail
- Tie it to the drainpipe, out of the current. -> tie
- Take the candles. Wax trades well at the Pumpline. -> take

> sfx splash
(The raft swings free, drifting up the flooded road toward the Wall.)
> set shrine_freed
> rep church 1
> approve ignace 2 tova 1
> goto close

(The water takes your feet. The raft bumps the wall once more.)
> end

> approve courtesy 1
> goto close

> give candle 3
> set shrine_taken

(A dark raft bumps the school wall.)
> end

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- One Sock (spin)

== niche_dryer
> if dryer_done -> after
(The last dryer in the row is spinning. A single red sock circles behind the glass.)
> if dryer_seen -> ask
> set dryer_seen
> quest dryer 1
TECH: The plug is severed. It powers nothing. It has spun for years.
> ifnot party:quaye -> ask
Quaye: Nine years. A kid woke it, sulking over a sock. I sleep to its rhythm.
* ask
> ifnot item:redsock -> leave
?
- Put the other sock in. -> sock
- Keep the sock. Let it keep turning. -> keep
- Roos buys socks in pairs. Take the red one out and sell both. -> sell
* sock
> take redsock
(The door swings open on its own. The sock goes in. The door clicks shut.)
(Two red socks circle once. Twice. The drum slows.)
> wait 2
(It stops. The door cracks open. The socks are warm.)
> set dryer_stopped
> approve courtesy 2
> ifnot party:quaye -> close
Quaye: Quiet, then.
> approve quaye -1
> goto close
* keep
(The sock slips back into your pocket. The dryer turns.)
> set dryer_kept
> ifnot party:quaye -> close
Quaye: Good.
> approve quaye 2
> goto close
* sell
> take redsock
> sfx shove
(The door resists you. Then it releases the sock.)
(The drum spins empty, faster.)
> gilt 6
> set dryer_robbed
> approve courtesy -2 quaye -2
* close
> set dryer_done
> quest dryer done
> end
* leave
> end
* after
> if dryer_stopped -> still
> if dryer_robbed -> empty
(One red sock goes round and round.)
> end
* still
(The dryer is still. Two red socks lie on top, folded.)
> end
* empty
(The drum spins, empty and unceasing.)
> end
