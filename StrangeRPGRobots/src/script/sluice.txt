# Sluice card tables. Each map with a table runs its scene. `> cards opponent -> win | lose` plays a game.

== sluice_market
? A card table. Sluice, five cards a side.
- Play Moss. -> moss
- Play Gus. -> gus
- Not now. -> no
* no
> end
* moss
Moss: Five cards. Higher side takes the square.
Moss: Ties do nothing. Don't argue with me about it.
> cards moss -> mw | ml
* mw
Moss: Fine. Take the card. I've got more in the tin.
> end
* ml
Moss: See you tomorrow. Bring better cards.
> end
* gus
Gus: Two puddles on the board tonight. A puddle gives a card plus one.
> cards gus -> gw | gl
* gw
Gus: Hm. That one was my lucky Lockjaw.
> end
* gl
Gus: The taxi's still running if you want to cry on the way home.
> end

== sluice_lock
? Bram keeps a deck in the tower.
- Deal. -> play
- Later. -> no
* no
> end
* play
Bram: Rule number one: after four cards, the bottom row floods.
Bram: Flooded cards lose one side. Just like in real life.
> cards bram -> w | l
* w
Bram: I've played this hand for thirty years. Go on, take one.
> end
* l
Bram: Down there, the water always wins. Up here, it's me.
> end

== sluice_spirebase
