# Ledger notes: what Nodus writes about each foe and each recorded attack, in clerk shorthand.
# == note_<enemy> is the foe's page. == entry_<attack> is the recorded attack's line.

== note_pebbler
Pebbler. Throws stones. Shadow lands first, stone lands second.
Good arm. No aim. Aims anyway.

== note_leadhound
Shadowhound. Runs down its own shadow and bites what's at the end.
Mind the end of the shadow. The middle's fine.

== note_gnat
Gnats. Too small to cast a shadow.
Nobody knows where they go next. Including them.

== note_shalecrab
Shale crab. Walks sideways. Pinches sideways.
Pinches three tiles at once if you let it.

== note_lanternman
Office lantern man. Carries a licensed light.
Shadows near him point away from it. So do mine.

== note_grisaille
Bailiff Grisaille. Rule nine. Rule thirty. Lantern.
Struck me from the register. Neat handwriting.

== note_hatman
Hat man. Wide brim. His shadow stays under it.
You see what he does when he's next to you. Too late, usually.

== note_inkmite
Ink mite. Paints a shadow where nothing goes.
Hard edge. Ignore it.

== note_cutpurse
Cutpurse. Pounces, grabs, runs.
Followed his shadow once. It was painted.

== note_lampthief
Lamp thief. Hoods other people's lamps.
Hates Parhelia. Everyone who meets Parhelia has opinions.

== note_painter
The Painter. Talks in colors.
Paints where you'll go. Check the edges.

== note_glareback
Glareback. Beetle with a mirror back.
Spits glare toward the light. Nothing else does.

== note_suntick
Sun tick. Tiny. No shadow.
Bites ankles. Unforeseen ankles.

== note_linesman
Relay linesman. Mirror shield.
First hit bounces. Second doesn't.

== note_heatwisp
Heat wisp. Lives on hot tiles.
Shade puts it out. It hates that.

== note_tallman
The Tall Man. Shadow as long as a street, even here.
Read four of his moves. All four were bad for me.

== note_heliotrope
Heliotrope. Faces the brightest light.
Bring it a brighter light than the one it wants.

== note_miragecat
Mirage cat. Its body stands a tile off from itself.
Trust the shadow. Never the cat.

== note_silverfish
Silverfish. Late.
Whatever it did last round, it does again.

== note_panehound
Pane hound. Carries a pane in its teeth.
Bites twice, the second bite a round late.

== note_fusehulk
Fuse hulk. Glass, melted, walking.
Slow. Very heavy. Very slow.

== note_warden
Warden of Panes. Office frame.
Twelve of it in the glass. One shadow.

== note_gambler
Overhead gambler. No shadow, no plan.
Would not promise to stop hitting me.

== note_noonbird
Noon bird. Drops straight down. No warning.
Look up. That doesn't help. Look up anyway.

== note_bearer
Office Bearer. Licensed lamp.
His lamp makes shadows around him. Mine too, if I stand near.

== note_keeper
Keeper of the First Mirror.
Wants Parhelia's lamp out. Wants the room to stay blind.

== note_feeler
Feeler. Blind. Hits whatever moved last.
Stand still. Make someone else move.

== note_lightdrinker
Light drinker. Sips lanterns.
The circle gets smaller every time it drinks.

== note_crawler
Dark crawler. Only there when lit.
I prefer it unlit. Can't hit it unlit.

== note_oldstray
The Old Stray. A shadow with no body, very old, very big.
Wanted mine. Couldn't have it.

== note_lampeater
Lampeater. Eats lights. Sleeps when full.
Fed it the lantern. Hit it in the dark. Tik heard where.

== note_paintedlead
Painted shadow, walking. Office work.
Hard edges. It's only paint. It still hurts.

== note_anthelion
Director Anthelion. Licenses every light.
Never lied to me once.

== note_greatlamp
The Great Lamp. A house of glass and sun.
Nobody hoods the sun. Two lamps on a mirror, though.
