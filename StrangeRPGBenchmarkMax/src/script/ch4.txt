# Chapter 4: Late Glass
# Maps: glass_flats, glass_well, glass_quarry.
# Sand fused to green glass under a sun nearly overhead. Every surface shows the past, a little late.

== ch4_open
> lock
> chapter 4 Late Glass
> music glass
> fade in
> wait 0.6
Umbraculum: The ground's glass!! My foot is a magnificent foot but it's not a glass foot!!
Parhelia: Don't look down too long. The glass shows what was here. (Rude to stare at the past.)
> set ch4_started
> unlock
> end

== tut_late
(Glass shows things a moment late. So do mirrors.)
> end

== mirage_caravan
(A mirage crosses the dune: a long line of people with lamps, walking toward the sun.)
(Their leads are short and pale. The glass is older than any of them.)
> end

> emote parhelia ...
> end
* seen
> end

== mirage_cart
(A mirage of a cart loaded with empty frames. An Office seal on the side.)
(The mirage is newer than the others. The colors haven't faded much.)
> end

== talk_parhelia_glass
> if ch4_mirage_bearers -> bearers
Parhelia: Glass gets everywhere. In your boots, in your past.
> end
* bearers
Parhelia: That mirage by the rocks was my brother and me. Before he was the Director.
Parhelia: We shared a lamp for a year. (He held it better.)
> end

== ch4_minim_meet
> lock
> music minim
> face minim nodus
Minim: You're late.
Parhelia: You're one of the Silvered.
Minim: One of the Silvered. Yes.
Minim: Minim. From the quarry. Walked out last sleep.
Umbraculum: SALUTATIONS, glass lady!! I am Lord Umbraculum Footwide!!
Minim: Footwide Umbraculum Lord. Salutations, glass lady.
Umbraculum: I am not a glass lady.
Minim: No. I am.
?
- What's in the quarry?
- Noted.
Minim: Rows of us. Office stands us facing the sun, turns us when it moves.
Minim: It doesn't move. So they don't turn us. Just stand.
Minim: Stand long enough, you forget walking's a thing.
Lot: Walk. Now.
Minim: Now walk. Yes. I'll show you the way in.
> join minim
> set ch4_minim_joined
> unlock
> end

== tut_pane
(Minim sets a pane on a tile. An attack whose lead crosses it runs again next round.)
(Recall puts an ally's HP back to what it was at the start of the last round.)
> end

== tut_mirage

(Trust its lead. A lead is never a mirage.)
> end

== ch4_warden

> camera @warden 1.0
