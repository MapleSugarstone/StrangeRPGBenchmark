# Strays: twelve loose leads, each waiting somewhere dark for an owner who lost it.
# stray_find_N runs when the party touches the stray. stray_return_N runs when the party talks to the owner while carrying it.
# A stray needs Lot to be carried. Without Lot, the find scene says so and the stray stays put.

== stray_nolot
(A small stray lies pinned in the shade. It won't come with you.)
(Something that lives in shade could carry it.)
> end

== stray_find_1
(A stray in the back of the quarry tunnel, shaped like a person with a pick.)
Lot: Lost. Waiting. I carry.
> end

== stray_return_1
Dunnet: My lead! Left it in the quarry tunnel nine years ago. Dark in there.
Dunnet: Walking around not knowing anything for nine years. Thanks, clerk.
> end
